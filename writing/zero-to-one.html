ZERO TO ONE · TEMPLATE-BASED WRITING

0-1｜有模板前提下，写出一篇新作文

这里不是新增一套万能模板，也不是把范文堆在一起。我们以你自己写过、已经练熟的模板为起点：先判断题型，再选择模板；保留原有骨架，替换题目内容；把每一个逻辑台阶推完整；最后完成整篇作文。每一篇文章都是一堂可以单独打开的课。

本课程的固定教学顺序

题目与图片信息 → 题型判断 → 选择你已有的模板 → 标出保留的模板原句 → 填入本题内容 → 补齐因果链 → 逐句拼接 → 完整范文 → 同底层逻辑迁移练习。

先学会一件事：不是每道题都套同一个模板

模板是组织思路的支架，不是把任何题目硬塞进同一套句子。先识别画面关系，再决定调用哪一套。

正向现象／价值类暗夸模板

画面呈现值得肯定的行为或品质。由时代背景、普遍行为、个人收益逐步上升到社会价值。

负面现象／社会问题类讽刺模板

先指出不良现象，再写直接危害、进一步影响、价值冲突，最后提出个人与社会层面的改进。

双主体／双视角类对比模板

两个人面对相同情境却采取不同做法。由差异推原因，再说明正确态度与行动的意义。

图表／图画＋图表类图表模板

先读数据、筛选重点、说明图表关系，再从外因和内在需求解释趋势，最后回应数据揭示的问题。

每篇课都必须回答的四个问题
  1. 为什么选这套模板，而不是另外几套？
  2. 模板中哪些句子原样保留，哪些位置必须换内容？
  3. 从一个观点到下一个观点，中间缺少什么因果台阶？
  4. 这篇作文写完后，哪些句型和思路可以迁移到下一篇？

统一的“从0到1”流程

① 看题：只提取证据

把人物、动作、场景、对比、图下注释、图表对象、单位、年份和数据逐项记录。画面没有的信息不能自行补充。

② 定型：先看关系，再看主题

看正负、单主体还是双主体、静态还是趋势、单图还是组合图。主题词不能替代题型判断。

③ 选模板：保留稳定骨架

把模板原句放在左边，本题要填的内容放在右边。模板不是全部重写，只在必要位置替换。

④ 推理：补全中间台阶

行为 → 直接影响 → 对个人/他人的影响 → 长期结果 → 社会意义。不是每篇都要写满所有层级，但每一步都要有因果依据。

⑤ 成文：先清楚，再准确

先写主干句，再添加原因、结果、对比和修饰。避免为了高级而写自己无法掌控的长句。

⑥ 迁移：换题不换思维

最后抽出可复用的句型和逻辑，而不是只背这一篇范文。

你的原有模板：先保留，再训练迁移

以下按你原来材料中的框架整理。原始模板是学习的基准；后续范文会明确标注哪些是模板原句、哪些是为适配题目而替换的内容。

模板一｜正向现象类／暗夸模板

The picture presents us with a thought-provoking scene: we can see [描述画面主体动作/场景].
Below the picture, a sentence reads "[引用图下文字]".
The cartoon vividly reflects [主题]. Today, with the acceleration of [时代背景], people attach greater significance to [主题]. It is quite normal for [主人公] to [具体正向行为]. This is like [美妙的比喻], and [核心主题词] has also become a spiritual treasure for us. It not only provides people with a simple way to [浅层好处], but also brings [深层好处] to our society.
In my opinion, we should advocate and carry forward [核心主题词] with a grateful heart. At the same time, we should translate it into concrete actions rather than empty slogans. If we do so, [回扣图画意象] will be more [形容词], and it will help us create a better future.

使用条件：画面确实呈现正向行为、品质或价值。spiritual treasure、with a grateful heart 等强褒义表达不能机械套到中性或负面题目。

模板二｜讽刺类／社会问题类

In the picture, [场景描述]. Around the [场景], [具体细节].
The picture reflects [不良现象], which is also a moral problem / a common social problem. [具体行为] not only [危害一] but also [危害二]. Worse still, such behavior may [进一步危害]. In fact, these people [行为] at the cost of [被牺牲的事物], putting [错误优先级] above [正确价值]. More importantly, this is not just a personal problem/habit but a lack of [公共道德/社会关注].
Therefore, we should firmly resist such behaviors and [行动]. [社会主体] should also [措施]. Only in this way can we [积极结果].

模板三｜双主体／双视角对比模板

The picture presents us with a thought-provoking scene: [整体画面]. In contrast, [两人不同表现]. One [表现A], while the other [表现B].
The picture emphasizes that [主题] is important. [两种不同表现的对比] result from [核心原因]. When [面临同一情境], one [视角A], while the other [视角B]. Therefore, we should [行动建议]. It does not directly guarantee [终极目标], but it makes people more likely to [积极作用]. People with such a [特质] tend to [结果]. As shown above, [总结句].
In conclusion, [主题] matters greatly when we face [困境/情境]. Thus, we should [行动建议], just as the [正面人物] in the drawing does.

使用条件：图中确实有两个主体，或同一主体的两种清晰对照。若画面没有对比，不为了套模板而虚构第二个人。

模板四｜图表作文骨架

The chart(s) clearly illustrate [对象/主题]. Overall, [总体趋势/主要特征]. Specifically, [关键数据一], while [关键数据二].
Several factors can account for [现象/变化]. First and foremost, [外部因素]. More importantly, it is not just about [表层需求], but about pursuing a better life. [主体] now attach greater importance to [深层需求].
In essence, this trend reflects [本质意义]. To maintain/improve [积极趋势/现状], [相关主体] should [建议]. Only through joint efforts can we [社会目标].

图表模板的关键不是把百分比抄一遍，而是挑出能支撑总体判断的代表数据，并解释图表之间的关系。

预测②｜图画＋图表组合题：“今年第三次来了！”

图画＋图表图表模板
原资料目前能确认的内容

上传文件第2页确认了作文要求：描述图画和图表、解释寓意、给出评论；图下注释是“今年第三次来了！”。但当前可读取文字没有识别出画面对象、图表标题和数值，不能猜成博物馆或其他具体场景。

第一段的固定分工

图画负责给出一个具体人物/行为/场景；图表负责提供群体层面的数量、比例或趋势。两者可能是“个体例子＋整体数据”，但必须先确认图表统计对象，才能说明它们是否相互印证。

可沿用的拼接骨架

The picture presents us with a scene in which [图画可见事实]. The accompanying chart further illustrates [图表统计对象]. Overall, [总体趋势]. Specifically, [关键数据一], while [关键数据二].

第二段怎样找共同主题

先判断图画中的“第三次来”表达重复参与、重复消费还是重复体验；再看图表是否呈现同一行为的变化。只有两者指向同一对象，才写“图画是图表趋势的具体例子”。如果图表与图画只是并列信息，就分别解释后再找共同主题，不能编造因果。

高清原图和数据核实后，再补充数字、模板填空和完整范文。

2026真题｜养老机器人：双图表作文从0到1

图表模板外因驱动＋内因升级
这篇仍然沿用你已有的图表模板

材料明确说明：2026首次考查双图表，但“外因驱动＋内因升级”的逻辑支架仍然适用。第一段换数据，第二段沿用原因链，第三段把建议对准最大痛点。

① 读数据：先算总体，再抓极值

图表数据准确结论
接受度完全接受39.3%；部分接受32.8%；不接受27.9%39.3%＋32.8%=72.1%，超过七成“不同程度接受”，不是七成完全接受
关注点安全46.3%；价格24.9%；便利10.7%安全是最大关注点

② 第一段：把数字放进模板

The two charts clearly illustrate consumers' attitudes toward elderly-care robots and their top concerns. Overall, it is obvious that over 70% of respondents hold a positive attitude toward this technology. Specifically, 39.3% fully accept these robots, while 32.8% show partial acceptance. Without exception, safety takes up the largest proportion in concerns, accounting for 46.3%.

顺序：图表主题 → 总体态度 → 饼图核心数据 → 柱状图最大项。不是把每个百分比机械抄一遍。

③ 第二段：外因驱动＋内因升级＋例证

外因：为什么社会需要？

First and foremost, driven by the accelerating aging population, there is a huge demand for caregiving support.

老龄化加速 → 照护需求增加。

内因：人们真正追求什么？

More importantly, it is not just about buying a smart device, but about pursuing a better life. Families and elderly people now attach greater importance to independent living and care quality.

不只是买设备，而是追求更好的生活；进一步落到独立生活与照护质量。

例证：技术怎样回应需求？

A case in point is the intelligent caregiving robot, which brings people convenience and safety, and has turned from a luxury into a practical helper for ordinary families.

用养老机器人把抽象需求落到具体产品上。

④ 第三段：建议回应数据痛点

In essence, this trend is a mirror of technological progress and the improvement of elderly care. To maintain this positive trend, the government should not only support the industry but also set strict safety standards. Only through joint efforts can we build a more harmonious and prosperous society.

46.3%最关注安全 → 建议设置严格安全标准。数据、问题、建议首尾对应。

迁移规律

新双图表题：第一段换成新数据；第二段判断能否沿用外因＋内因；第三段从图表中找最大痛点，让建议精准回应它。

2013真题｜毕业生人生岔路口：双主体模板

双主体／双视角

① 为什么选这套模板？

你材料中的范文把画面组织为一群毕业生站在人生岔路口，面对求职、深造、创业等道路，并进一步写出两名毕业生面对同一岔路口的不同反应。正是“同一处境、不同反应”让双主体模板适用。

② 模板句如何填充

原模板本题内容
The picture presents us with a thought-provoking scene...a group of graduates are standing at a crossroads of life...
In contrast...the graduates have different reactions to the same crossroad
One..., while the other...one strides forward confidently... while the other hesitates...

③ 第二段逻辑链

差异

一个目标明确，另一个犹豫迷茫。

原因

不同的自我认知导致两人对未来和自身能力的判断不同。

具体化思维差异

When facing the unknown future, one sees clear targets, while the other sees insurmountable obstacles.

建议

Therefore, we should consciously cultivate the ability to make independent decisions based on our own strengths.

辩证结果

It does not directly guarantee immediate success, but it makes people more likely to find their true calling and avoid blind conformity.

不保证立即成功，但更可能找到适合自己的方向并避免盲从。

④ 完整范文

The picture presents us with a thought-provoking scene: a group of graduates are standing at a crossroads of life, facing various paths such as job hunting, further study, and starting a business. In contrast, the graduates have different reactions to the same crossroad. One strides forward confidently with clear goals, while the other hesitates at the intersection, feeling confused and anxious about the future.

The picture emphasizes that making rational and independent choices is important. The graduates' different reactions to the same crossroad result from different levels of self-awareness. When facing the unknown future, one sees clear targets, while the other sees insurmountable obstacles. Therefore, we should consciously cultivate the ability to make independent decisions based on our own strengths. It does not directly guarantee immediate success, but it makes people more likely to find their true calling and avoid blind conformity. People with such an ability tend to enjoy the exploring process and achieve better career development. As shown above, rational people are more likely to find the suitable path for themselves.

In conclusion, wise choices matter greatly when we face life's turning points. Thus, we should evaluate ourselves objectively and learn to make independent decisions, just as the determined graduates in the drawing do.

骨架递进：In contrast对比 → result from追因 → When...one...while the other...具体解释 → Therefore建议 → It does not guarantee...but...辩证结果 → People with such...tend to...长期结果。

所有作文共通的底层逻辑

题材会变化，模板会切换，但写作时始终在完成几项相同的思维任务。

共同主线：材料 → 关系 → 意义 → 判断 → 行动

1. 材料：我看到了什么？

图画给出人物和行为，图表给出对象和数据。先忠实描述，不抢先解释。

2. 关系：材料之间有什么联系？

可能是对比、因果、递进、变化、并列或个体与群体的对应。关系决定文章结构。

3. 意义：为什么值得讨论？

从具体行为或数据变化出发，解释它对个人、他人或社会意味着什么。

4. 判断：我赞成什么、反对什么？

正向题肯定价值，问题题指出代价，对比题解释差异，图表题解释趋势和关注点。

5. 行动：应该怎样做？

建议必须回应前文指出的原因或问题，形成闭环，而不是突然喊口号。
