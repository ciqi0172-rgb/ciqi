GUIDED WRITING COURSE · STEP BY STEP

不是看理论，是跟着老师把句子写出来

每一课都按“看题 → 找信息 → 选语料 → 拼句子 → 嵌入模板 → 完整成文”展开。你不需要自己对着一堆理论摸索，我会把每个决定拆给你看：为什么选这个词，为什么这样排序，为什么这一句能接下一句。

LESSON 01 · PICTURE DESCRIPTION

第一段：先学会把画面说清楚

第一段的任务不是提前讲道理，而是让阅卷人准确看到画面。最稳妥的顺序是：整体场景 → 主体与动作 → 关键细节/对比 → 图下注释（如果有）。不要把第二段的主题分析提前塞进来。

一、第一段的“积木语料库”

1. 开头：把读者带进画面

The picture presents us with a thought-provoking scene:图片向我们呈现了一个发人深省的场景：适合你原模板，后面直接接具体画面。
In the picture, we can see...在图画中，我们可以看到……最直接、最稳，不容易写错。
The cartoon depicts...这幅漫画描绘了……适合直接概括场景。
The drawing portrays a scene in which...这幅图描绘了这样一个场景……适合后面接完整情境。

2. 人物：谁在画面里

a young man / a young woman一名年轻男子 / 女子
a group of students / graduates一群学生 / 毕业生
an elderly person / an old couple一位老人 / 一对老年夫妇
two people from different generations来自不同代际的两个人
some volunteers一些志愿者
people from different backgrounds来自不同背景的人们

3. 动作：人物正在做什么

is holding / are holding正拿着、握着
is offering a helping hand正在伸出援手
are gathered around...围坐 / 聚集在……周围
are staring at their smartphones正盯着智能手机
is standing at a crossroads正站在人生的十字路口
is throwing rubbish into...正把垃圾扔进……
is reading a book / taking notes正在读书 / 做笔记
is talking with / listening to...正在与……交谈 / 倾听……
are working together on...正在共同完成……
is taking part in a volunteer activity正在参加志愿活动

4. 位置与细节：把画面交代完整

in the center of the picture在画面中央
on the left / right在左侧 / 右侧
in front of / behind...在……前面 / 后面
around the table围着桌子
with... in front of them他们面前有……（with 结构补充背景）
with a smile on his / her face脸上带着微笑
while nobody is...而没有人在……（突出反差）
instead of...而不是……（强调实际情况与预期不同）
surrounded by...被……环绕
both... and...两者都……（并列画面信息）

5. 图下注释与文字

Below the picture, a sentence reads “[图下注释原文].”
图片下方有一句话：“……”——有明确文字时才用，原文照录，不要自行改写引号内容。

二、老师示范：一个句子是怎么“拼”出来的

示范 A：2015 年聚会低头族（先看见什么，就写什么）

先提取画面信息

人物：a group of friends；地点：around a table；细节：a meal；动作：everyone looks at phones；反差：nobody talks。

先写最简单的主干

A group of friends are sitting around a table.

主语 + are sitting，先把谁在哪里交代清楚。

补充背景：桌上有什么

A group of friends are sitting around a table, with a delicious meal served in front of them.

with 结构补充画面细节，不需要再另起一句。

补充真正关键的动作

Around the table, everyone is staring at their smartphones.

stare at 比 look at 更能表达“盯着看”。

用 while 写出反差

..., while nobody is talking or communicating with each other.

聚会本应交流，但画面里没有交流。这一反差是后面主题分析的依据。

A group of friends are sitting around a table, with a delicious meal served in front of them. Around the table, everyone is staring at their smartphones, while nobody is talking or communicating with each other.

注意：第一段只写“大家盯着手机、没有交流”，不要提前写“这反映了人际关系疏远”。后者是第二段的主题判断。

示范 B：文化火锅（画面物品 + 特殊之处 + 图下注释）

先说看到的主体

We can see a boiling hot pot.

一个正在沸腾的火锅。

用 however 引出不寻常之处

However, its ingredients are not traditional food...

关键不是普通食材，而是文化概念，所以要写出反常之处。

把食材具体化

...but some cultural concepts from China and abroad.

从中国和国外来的文化观念。这里不需要擅自编造图中没有的具体文化符号。

补上图下注释

Below the picture, a sentence reads “Cultural hot pot is both delicious and nutritious.”

The picture presents us with a thought-provoking scene: we can see a boiling hot pot. However, its ingredients are not traditional food but some cultural concepts from China and abroad. Below the picture, a sentence reads “Cultural hot pot is both delicious and nutritious.”

示范 C：毕业生站在人生岔路口

先说主体和位置

A group of graduates are standing at a crossroads.

补充他们面对的选项

...facing several choices, including pursuing further education, studying abroad, starting a business and looking for a job.

最后用一句概括情境

They seem to be faced with an important decision about their future.

这句是对画面信息的概括，不替他们判断哪条路更好。

The picture presents us with a thought-provoking scene: a group of graduates are standing at a crossroads, facing several choices, including pursuing further education, studying abroad, starting a business and looking for a job. They seem to be faced with an important decision about their future.

三、第一段最容易犯的三个错误

① 把主题分析提前写进第一段；② 为了显得丰富，编造人物身份、表情、数据或背景；③ 把每个细节都写进去，导致句子过长。我们只选能支撑主题的关键细节。
LESSON 02 · POSITIVE PICTURE

正向图画：从具体行为写到社会意义

这类题目不是简单夸一句“很好”。我们要把行为写具体，再解释它满足了什么需求、带来什么变化，最后上升到更广的价值。你原来的正向模板可以大范围沿用。

一、正向图画主题语料：先从“行为”入手

Filial piety / family bonds孝道 / 家庭纽带；行为：hold aged parents' hands, offer companionship, spend time with family.
Cultural integration文化融合；行为：experience different cultures, appreciate cultures from all times and places.
Personal development个人发展；行为：learn new skills, take part in activities, improve one's abilities.
The habit of reading阅读习惯；行为：read books regularly, visit libraries, take online courses.
Physical health身体健康；行为：jog in the park, go to the gym, play basketball with friends.
The spirit of dedication奉献精神；行为：teach children in rural areas, help the elderly, donate to charities.
Social responsibility社会责任；行为：participate in public welfare, offer a helping hand, maintain public order.
The courage to try尝试的勇气；行为：test ideas in real life, apply theory to practice, try new methods.

二、示范：文化融合的第二段怎样一步步搭出来

先命名主题

The cartoon vividly reflects cultural integration.

从火锅里放入中外文化元素，最直接的主题是文化融合，而不是笼统的“文化很重要”。

把时代背景与观念变化连起来

Today, with the acceleration of cultural fusion, people attach greater significance to cultural exchange.

这里先写社会背景，再写人们越来越重视什么。注意 cultural fusion 与 cultural integration 语义相近，但不是机械重复。

把抽象观念落到具体行为

It is quite normal for a person to experience different cultures.

不要只说 people value diversity。具体到“人们愿意体验不同文化”，文章就有内容了。

补充一个可视化的比喻

This is like putting different elements into the cultural hot pot...

直接拿第一段的火锅意象来解释文化融合，形成前后连接。

写出两层价值

They not only provide people with a simple way to see the rich and colorful world, but also bring new ways of thinking to people from different backgrounds.

浅层：看见丰富世界；深层：形成新的思维方式。后者比“让生活更丰富”更具体。

The cartoon vividly reflects cultural integration. Today, with the acceleration of cultural fusion, people are more likely to experience different cultures. Moreover, it is quite normal for a person to appreciate cultures from all times and places, both ancient and modern, Chinese and foreign. This is like putting different elements into the cultural hot pot, and cultures have also become a spiritual treasure for us. Diverse cultures enrich people's lives. They not only provide people with a simple way to see the rich and colorful world, but also bring new ways of thinking to people from different backgrounds.

三、再看其他正向主题如何换“肉”

主题具体行为浅层收获深层意义
孝道与亲情offer companionship to aged parentsmake elderly people feel cared forstrengthen family bonds
学习与阅读read books regularly / learn new skillsbroaden one's horizonspromote continuous personal growth
运动与健康exercise regularly / play sportskeep fit and release pressurebuild a healthy and energetic society
志愿服务help the elderly / teach childrenmake a difference in others' livesbuild a caring and warm society
创新实践apply theory to practice / try new methodssolve problems more effectivelypromote innovation and social development

四、第三段：不要只重复主题，要把图画收回来

文化火锅的闭环

In my opinion, we should welcome diverse cultures with an open mind. At the same time, we should keep our own cultural roots. If we do so, the cultural hot pot will be more delicious and nutritious, and it will help us create a better future.

前面讨论文化融合，结尾回到火锅的“美味、营养”；同时补上“保持文化根基”，使建议不是单向度地接受外来文化。

你要记住：结尾呼应的关键，不是再说一次 cultural integration，而是把第一段独有的图画意象转化成积极结果。
LESSON 03 · NEGATIVE PICTURE

负面图画：从一个行为写出真正的问题

讽刺类不能套正向“暗夸”。要先点明不良行为，再写直接危害、进一步后果、行为背后的价值取舍，最后上升到公共责任。

一、第一步：把动作变成问题主题

2015 聚会低头族

The picture reflects young people's over-indulgence in smartphones, which is also a common social problem.

不是只说“people use phones”，而是指出“过度沉迷手机”。主题词要包含问题性质。

直接危害一：破坏聚会氛围

Playing with phones not only ruins the warm atmosphere of gatherings...

直接危害二：疏远人与人之间的距离

...but also widens the distance between people.

进一步后果

Worse still, such a habit may make people feel increasingly lonely.

揭示代价与错误优先级

In fact, these people immerse themselves in the virtual world at the cost of real communication, putting their virtual experience above real feelings.

上升到公共层面

More importantly, this is not just a personal habit but a lack of social attention to real communication.

The picture reflects young people's over-indulgence in smartphones, which is also a common social problem. Playing with phones not only ruins the warm atmosphere of gatherings but also widens the distance between people. Worse still, such a habit may make people feel increasingly lonely. In fact, these people immerse themselves in the virtual world at the cost of real communication, putting their virtual experience above real feelings. More importantly, this is not just a personal habit but a lack of social attention to real communication.

二、2011 乱扔垃圾：同一骨架，换成公共环境

行为 + 两个直接影响

Throwing rubbish everywhere in tourist attractions not only damages the environment but also affects other visitors' experience.

递进后果

Worse still, such behavior may encourage others to follow this bad example.

代价 + 价值排序

These people enjoy their journey at the cost of the public environment, putting their own convenience above public interests.

上升到公共道德

More importantly, this is not just a personal problem but a lack of public morality.

你会发现，2011 和 2015 的内容不同，但 In fact... at the cost of... putting... above... 这一逻辑骨架可以统一背诵。我们不是换一篇新文章，而是在同一骨架里换被牺牲的东西和错误优先级。
LESSON 04 · CHART INTERPRETATION

图表作文：数据不是摆设，它必须服务于主题

图表作文和图画作文的第一段不同：图画写场景，图表写数据关系。但后面的核心问题一样——你必须从数据中找到一个可靠的中心判断，再解释它意味着什么。我们沿用你已经背过的“外因驱动 + 内因升级”模板。

一、读图先做四件事

看图表对象

谁的数据？消费者、大学生、居民，还是其他群体？时间范围是什么？

找总体特征

上升、下降、稳定、差异明显，还是多数人持某种态度？不要逐项抄数字。

挑关键数据

通常选总体占比、最高项、最低项、变化最大项。只选能支持中心判断的数据。

问数据说明什么

这组数据能支持什么主题？哪些结论不能从数据推出？把描述和推断分开。

二、趋势图：从数据写成英文

假设图表显示某项比例从 2022 年的 25% 上升到 2024 年的 70%，随后在 2025 年回落至 55%。

第 1 步｜先写主语

The proportion of [某群体 / 某行为]...

第 2 步｜写趋势动词

...rose sharply...

第 3 步｜写起点和终点

...from 25% in 2022 to 70% in 2024...

第 4 步｜补充峰值与回落

..., reached a peak of 70%, before falling to 55% in 2025.

The proportion of [某群体 / 某行为] rose sharply from 25% in 2022 to 70% in 2024, reached a peak of 70%, before falling to 55% in 2025.

这是趋势表达的拼接示范。实际写作时注意避免 reached a peak of 70% 与前面 to 70% 重复，可以改成：rose sharply from 25% in 2022 to a peak of 70% in 2024, before falling to 55% in 2025.

rose / increased / climbed上升；climbed 常用于数字逐步攀升
fell / declined / dropped下降；dropped 可强调下降幅度或速度
remained stable保持稳定
fluctuated波动
reached a peak of...达到……峰值
accounted for...占……比例
ranked first / came second排名第一 / 位居第二
stood at...数值为……

三、双图表示范：2026 养老机器人接受度与关注点

这道题最重要的不是把所有数字念一遍，而是看懂两张图的分工：第一张回答“消费者是否接受”，第二张回答“消费者最关心什么”。两张图合起来，才能形成有层次的主题判断。

图表信息关键数据可支持的判断
完全接受39.3%有相当比例的受访者完全接受
部分接受32.8%部分接受者也占有明显比例
不接受27.9%仍有一部分人持保留态度
最关注安全46.3%安全是最突出的关注点
价格24.9%成本也是重要考虑
便利10.7%便利性受到关注，但低于安全和价格

数据怎么拼成中心判断？

第一张图：接受度

39.3% + 32.8% = 72.1%。因此可以说 over 70% of respondents show some degree of acceptance。不要误写成“72.1%完全接受”。

第二张图：关注点

Safety takes up the largest proportion, accounting for 46.3%。因此主题不能只写“科技很受欢迎”，还要看到安全是消费者的核心顾虑。

两张图合起来

一方面，消费者总体接受度较高；另一方面，他们尤其重视安全。这意味着技术推广有市场基础，但安全保障不可忽视。

四、把数据放进你的模板：逐句拼接

开头：交代双图主题

The two charts clearly illustrate consumers' attitudes toward elderly-care robots and their top concerns.

第一张图是态度，第二张图是关注点。

Overall：概括总体态度

Overall, it is obvious that over 70% of respondents show some degree of acceptance toward this technology.

72.1% 是完全接受 + 部分接受，不等于完全接受。

Specifically：选饼图关键数据

Specifically, 39.3% fully accept these robots, while 32.8% show partial acceptance.

Without exception：转向柱状图最高项

Safety takes up the largest proportion in consumers' concerns, accounting for 46.3%.

注意：Without exception 不是每道题都能用；这里更自然的写法是直接用 Safety...

The two charts clearly illustrate consumers' attitudes toward elderly-care robots and their top concerns. Overall, it is obvious that over 70% of respondents show some degree of acceptance toward this technology. Specifically, 39.3% fully accept these robots, while 32.8% show partial acceptance. Safety takes up the largest proportion in consumers' concerns, accounting for 46.3%.

五、第二段：数据如何服务于主题，而不是硬编原因

你的固定逻辑：外因 → 内因升级 → 案例

外因驱动

First and foremost, driven by the accelerating aging population, there is a huge demand for caregiving support.

老龄化是客观背景，解释为什么养老照护需求增加。

内因升级

More importantly, it is not just about buying a smart device, but about pursuing a better life.

从“买一个设备”提升到“追求更好的生活”。

具体需求

Families and elderly people now attach greater importance to independent living and care quality.

把 better life 解释清楚：独立生活与照护质量。

案例印证

A case in point is the intelligent caregiving robot, which brings people convenience and safety, and has turned from a luxury into a practical helper for ordinary families.

用机器人本身印证它如何回应需求；但别声称图表证明机器人已经普遍进入家庭。

六、第三段：本质意义 + 紧扣数据痛点的建议

In essence, this trend is a mirror of technological progress and the improvement of elderly care. To maintain this positive trend, the government should not only support the industry but also set strict safety standards. Only through joint efforts can we build a more harmonious and prosperous society.

为什么建议写安全标准？因为第二张图里安全占 46.3%，是最突出的关注点。建议必须回应数据，而不是随便喊“加强宣传”。

图表解读的核心：不是数据越多越好，而是每一个关键数据都要承担论证功能。第一张图给你“接受度”，第二张图给你“顾虑”，两者共同支撑“技术发展要回应真实照护需求，同时保障安全”。

七、大学生课余时间趋势：另一种图表逻辑

我们此前练过的那组数据，学习阅读与锻炼比例上升，单纯娱乐比例下降。这里不编造具体百分比，只根据趋势本身进行解读。

先描述变化

The proportions of students spending their leisure time on learning and exercise have increased, while the figure for pure entertainment has declined.

再提炼中心主题

The findings suggest that college students are attaching greater importance to personal development and physical and mental well-being.

数据不是只说明“课余活动变了”，而是支持学生开始更重视个人成长与身心健康。

再做内在需求升级

More importantly, it is not just about how college students spend their leisure time, but about pursuing a better life.

从“时间分配”提升到“生活质量与自我发展”。

LESSON 05 · TOPIC TRANSFER

其他题材：同一套方法，不同内容

我们不为每个主题重新背一篇作文。先判断题目类型，再从熟悉模板里提取可用逻辑，最后只换真正需要变化的内容。

题材 A：毕业生选择——抽象讨论类

不是双主体对比：画面是一群毕业生面对多条路，没有给出两种相反表现。也不是负面行为。用正向模板中的中性结构，但重写比喻和价值句。

主题句

The cartoon vividly reflects the importance of making wise choices in life.

背景 + 观念

Today, with the acceleration of social development, people attach greater significance to personal development and future career prospects.

普遍现象

It is quite normal for graduates to feel uncertain when facing several different paths after graduation.

巧妙转化图画意象

Making a choice is like choosing a direction at a crossroads.

这里不写 spiritual treasure，因为它带有赞美意味；用 crossroads / direction 承接图画。

建议 + 呼应

We should make our choices based on our interests and abilities... If we do so, we will be more likely to find a path that suits us.

题材 B：2007 点球寓言——双主体 / 双视角

两个人面对同一个球门，却有不同感知，所以可以沿用你双主体模板的逻辑。

对比信息

The different perceptions of the same challenge result from different attitudes toward difficulties.

同一情境、两种视角

When facing the same situation, one may focus too much on the size of the challenge, while the other may underestimate his own abilities.

辩证推进

It does not directly guarantee success, but it makes people more likely to overcome challenges.

结尾回扣

Confidence matters greatly when we face difficulties and challenges.

题材 C：大学生跨专业讲座 / 学习与知识积累

画面具体行为是参加讲座、接触不同领域；主题可落到知识积累、开阔视野和个人发展。

行为

It is quite normal for college students to attend lectures in different fields.

浅层价值

It provides students with a chance to learn about different fields and broaden their horizons.

深层价值

It also brings new ways of thinking and more possibilities for personal development.

升华但不空泛

More importantly, it is not just about attending a lecture, but about developing the ability to think from different perspectives.

题材 D：志愿服务 / 奉献

具体行为

It is quite normal for volunteers to offer a helping hand to people in need.

个人收获

Such activities make a difference in others' lives and give volunteers a sense of achievement.

社会意义

They also help build a caring and warm society.

结尾行动

We should take part in public welfare and turn our concern for others into concrete actions.

LESSON 06 · TEACHER-GUIDED PRACTICE

现在轮到你：老师带着你一步一步写

不要一下子写整篇。每次只完成一个小步骤。我把提示和可用语料放在旁边，你写完可以展开参考思路。答案不是唯一的，重点是你能解释自己的选择。

练习一：第一段——先把画面写成两个句子

假设图中是一位大学生坐在图书馆里读书，桌上有书本和笔记本。先写“人物 + 地点 + 动作”，再补充桌面细节。不要写主题分析。

第一步： a college student is reading a book in the library.
第二步： A college student is reading a book in the library, with several books and a notebook on the desk.

练习二：第二段——从行为找到两层好处

主题是阅读。请分别写一个浅层好处和一个深层好处。提示词：broaden one's horizons / improve one's knowledge / develop critical thinking / continuous personal growth。

浅层： broaden their horizons（开阔视野）。
深层： develop critical thinking（培养批判性思维）。
拼成句子： Reading not only broadens people's horizons, but also helps them develop critical thinking.
再迁移到模板： It not only provides people with a simple way to broaden their horizons, but also helps them develop critical thinking.

练习三：第三段——把建议和主题扣在一起

仍然是阅读主题。不要只写“we should read more”。尝试把阅读变成具体行动，并在结尾回扣“知识与成长”。

建议： In my opinion, we should make reading a daily habit.
补充行动： At the same time, we should stay curious about the world and never stop learning.
呼应： If we do so, reading will become a source of continuous personal growth, and it will help us create a better future.
思路：开头是读书行为，结尾把它转化成持续成长的来源。

自检：这次练习做到哪一步了？
